# Video Marketing

## Long-Form Script Structure

- Hook / Cold Open (0–30 sec): fulfill title/thumbnail promise quickly
- Intro: establish context and the central question
- Body: clear sections connected by cause/effect, contrast and real examples
- CTA only where it does not damage the explanation
- Outro: payoff/recap and callback when useful

## Principles

- strong material early
- clear audience promise
- spoken-language writing
- real examples over generic filler
- one main question per video
- vary sentence length and information density
- avoid fake hype

Project-specific rules in `CANONICAL_RULES.md` and the YouTube folder override generic advice.
