# STICKMAN DNA — STYLIZED HUMAN SOURCE OF TRUTH v2

**STATUS: HARD LOCK — CINEMATIC HUMAN BEHAVIOR WORLD v2**

Character system: `STYLIZED_STICKMAN_CAST_V2`

Anatomy sub-policy: `GEOMETRIC_STICKMAN_ANATOMY_V1`

## Core rule

Every visible person belongs to one polished illustrated Stickman family.

The target is **not** a bare line doodle and **not** a normal cartoon human. It is a simple, clean, hand-drawn editorial Stickman with enough shape for natural posing.

## Positive character DNA

- simple round or slightly oval head
- minimal expressive face: small eyes, simple mouth, optional simple eyebrows
- slim simplified torso
- thin clearly drawn arms and legs with a small consistent illustrated width
- limbs bend naturally at elbows and knees
- simple small hands and shoes
- simplified graphic clothing that follows the narrow Stickman body
- simple hair/accessory cues when useful
- clear readable silhouette

Do **not** force hairline arms/legs, triangle torsos or mathematically tiny bodies. The figure must remain easy to pose, sit, kneel, point, carry objects and interact naturally.

## Character expression

Use face, head angle, posture, gesture, gaze, spacing and pose to show emotion or social role.

Characters may be relaxed, curious, confident, insecure, anxious, isolated, dominant, skeptical, deceptive-friendly, exhausted or hopeful while staying inside the same Stickman family.

## Clothing

Clothing may be recognizable and may lightly shape the torso or limbs, but stays simple and graphic.

Prefer:
- one clear upper-body garment
- simple lower-body color/form
- small shoes
- one or two identity cues

Avoid turning clothing into realistic human anatomy or bulky mascot bodies.

## Background people

Background and crowd figures use the same Stickman family as main characters. They may be simpler, but they must not switch into realistic humans, generic corporate people or a different cartoon style.

## Topic adaptation

The Stickman family stays constant. The world around it adapts to the topic:
- bright/open for calm or constructive topics
- cooler/tighter for uncertainty
- darker/harder shadows for manipulation, danger or power
- warmer for hopeful or reflective scenes
- natural local colors for everyday scenes

The channel must not default every video to the same red/dark or teal/cream treatment.

## Continuity

When a character returns, preserve the same head shape, face language, clothing colors, silhouette, hair/accessory cues and overall proportions.

## Minimal hard reject

Reject only when the result clearly becomes:
- photorealistic or realistic human
- 3D/Pixar/anime
- cute/chibi/mascot
- bare whiteboard/classroom stick figure

## Compact style anchor

Use this short anchor in Flow image prompts when people appear:

`Same polished hand-drawn 2D Stickman style: simple round/oval heads, minimal expressive faces, slim simplified bodies, thin clearly drawn limbs, simple clothing, clean dark outlines, flat controlled colors and soft graphic shading.`
