## Cover strength

Use the same cinematic illustrated psychology world as normal scenes, but compose more strongly:
- one dominant emotional conflict/question
- larger focal subject
- readable silhouette
- stronger but believable color/light contrast
- clear foreground/midground/background when useful
- meaningful props
- safe title zone
- fast thumbnail readability

Do not switch to another comic, poster, 3D or flat-vector style just for the cover.
