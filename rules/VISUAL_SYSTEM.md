# VISUAL SYSTEM — CINEMATIC HUMAN BEHAVIOR WORLD v2

**STATUS: ACTIVE CHANNEL SOURCE OF TRUTH**

Policy ID: `CINEMATIC_HUMAN_BEHAVIOR_V2`

Character system: `STYLIZED_STICKMAN_CAST_V2`

Anatomy sub-policy: `GEOMETRIC_STICKMAN_ANATOMY_V1`

## Core idea

**One polished hand-drawn 2D Stickman illustration language, with a different scene world and mood for each topic.**

Characters and environments must look illustrated by the same artist. Do not mix simple Stickmen with semi-realistic rooms or product-render backgrounds.

## What stays constant

Across every video preserve:
- hand-drawn 2D illustration
- clean dark outlines
- simple readable shapes
- flat controlled colors
- soft graphic/cel shading
- light illustrated texture
- the same stylized Stickman family
- clear focal hierarchy

## Stickman family

Use `STYLIZED_STICKMAN_CAST_V2`:
- round or slightly oval heads
- minimal expressive faces
- slim simplified torsos
- thin clearly drawn arms and legs with enough width for natural posing
- simple hands and shoes
- simplified clothing and hair/accessory cues

Do not turn characters into bare line doodles or normal cartoon humans.

## Same illustrated language for the whole frame

Rooms, furniture, objects, streets, offices, cafés, stations and other environments use the **same simplified 2D illustration language** as the figures.

Recognizable detail is good. Photorealistic material rendering, realistic interior-render lighting and 3D-looking environments are not the target.

## Topic-specific world

Each video gets its own local world while keeping the same channel drawing DNA.

Examples:
- everyday/calm topic → brighter, open, natural colors
- uncertainty/anxiety → cooler, tighter, more negative space
- manipulation/power → darker values, stronger graphic shadows, controlled hierarchy
- money/status → suitable commercial/financial locations and restrained local colors
- isolation → sparse space and distance
- hope/solution → warmer, clearer, more open composition

Do not make every video red/dark. Do not make every video teal/cream.

## Human presence is optional

Use people when behavior, reaction or social relation is central.

Use object, environment, comparison, schema or text illustration when that communicates the beat faster. Do not force a Stickman into every image.

## Visual rule

One image = one clear visual thought.

Prefer concrete action, posture, gaze, distance, object relation and environment activity over abstract psychology symbols.

Named effects are not literal art instructions. Spotlight Effect does not automatically mean a spotlight; Halo Effect does not automatically mean a halo.

## Environment and composition

Use only useful props and enough depth to make the place readable. Backgrounds support the idea rather than compete with it.

Camera, viewpoint, scene density and lighting may vary strongly when the topic benefits from it.

## Color and light

Color and light follow the current beat. Keep them controlled and illustrated rather than photographic.

The focal subject gets the strongest useful contrast. Background detail and saturation may be reduced.

## Continuity

Recurring people, places and important props stay recognizable inside one video.

After the user chooses the cover, the approved `01.png` becomes the **video-local visual reference** for later batches: preserve its Stickman construction, linework, shading, texture and overall illustration quality. Do not copy its exact composition, room, pose or palette unless continuity requires it.

## Text

Visible text appears only when deliberately planned. Otherwise: `ZERO READABLE TEXT`.

## Minimal hard reject

Reject when the frame clearly drifts to:
- photorealism/realistic humans
- 3D/Pixar/anime
- cute/chibi/mascot
- bare whiteboard stick figures
- mixed realistic environment + simple doodle-character look
- random readable text
- unclear current beat
