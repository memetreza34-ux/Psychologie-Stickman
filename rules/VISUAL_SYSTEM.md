# VISUAL SYSTEM — CINEMATIC ILLUSTRATED PSYCHOLOGY WORLD v1

**STATUS: ACTIVE CHANNEL SOURCE OF TRUTH**

Policy ID: `CINEMATIC_ILLUSTRATED_PSYCHOLOGY_V1`

This system fully replaces former visual directions for NEW productions.

## 1. Core idea

**A consistent colorful 2D psychology film-world: one unified stylized stickman-inspired human family, believable locations, cinematic framing, soft cel shading and mixed visual storytelling.**

The channel is not a whiteboard channel and not a normal-cartoon-human channel. It should feel like frames from the same modern 2D animated psychology film.

## 2. Unified human cast — HARD LOCK

Whenever humans appear, **every visible human belongs to the same `STYLIZED_STICKMAN_CAST_V1` family**.

This includes:
- main character
- supporting characters
- background passengers/crowds
- coworkers, friends, family, strangers and researchers
- small people inside montages or memory inserts
- depicted humans on screens, posters, photos or reflections

Required family traits:
- large simple round or slightly oval head
- minimal expressive face
- compact simplified torso with visible clothing
- very slim simplified arms and legs with slight illustrated width
- simplified hands
- clear simplified shoes/feet
- readable pose and body language

Supporting/background people may be less detailed, but may **not** become normal cartoon humans, realistic people, generic vector people or another anatomy family.

Different individuals may vary through clothing, color, simple hair cue, glasses, accessories, height, posture and expression while remaining unmistakably the same character family.

## 3. Character presence is optional — HARD LOCK

**A strong video does not need a person in every frame.**

Choose humans only when human action/reaction is the clearest way to explain the beat.

Prefer no-human visuals when clearer:
- schema/mechanism
- symbolic object
- object close-up
- visual metaphor
- comparison
- environment-only illustration
- text illustration

Never add a decorative stickman merely because the frame feels empty.

No-human frames must still look like the same film-world through matching linework, color behavior, soft cel shading, depth and lighting.

## 4. World and environment

Locations should feel real enough to support the story:
- bedroom, apartment, café, train, supermarket, cinema, office, school, street, waiting room, restaurant, park and similar everyday places
- recognizable furniture, architecture and props
- small meaningful environmental details
- foreground, midground and background when useful
- natural perspective and spatial logic
- background may be simplified or softly subdued so it never competes with the main idea

The environment is allowed to be visually rich. It must not become random decoration.

## 5. Rendering and film feeling

Use:
- polished 2D animated-film illustration
- clean slightly organic dark outlines
- soft cel shading
- controlled texture
- believable shadows
- natural depth
- purposeful camera angle
- warm/cool light contrast when useful
- cinematic but still clearly illustrated lighting
- rich controlled colors

Never drift into:
- photorealism
- 3D rendering
- glossy CGI
- painterly concept art that no longer matches the channel
- flat corporate vector stock art
- whiteboard/doodle aesthetics

## 6. Color system

No forced monochrome channel palette.

Use believable scene colors with a controlled recurring channel character:
- deep navy / dark blue for night, depth and calm
- warm amber for lamps, safety and attention
- muted teal/blue for neutral focus
- coral/red for tension, warning or conflict
- natural local object colors

Rules:
- colorful, but not candy-colored
- warm/cool contrast may support emotion
- key object or character gets strongest contrast
- backgrounds may be quieter
- different locations may have different local colors while still sharing the same rendering and lighting language

## 7. Visual representation families

The same visual world may explain a beat in different ways.

### A — CHARACTER_SCENE
One or more people from the unified stickman family acting inside a believable location.

### B — SCHEMA_EXPLAINER
A clean illustrated mechanism with arrows, short labels, icons, cards or pathways. **Human optional.** It must look illustrated inside the same world, never like a corporate slide.

### C — OBJECT_METAPHOR
**No character required.** Use a symbolic object or environment to explain an invisible psychological mechanism: tangled knot, glowing phone, ticket, door, magnet, loop, scale, mirror, spotlight, etc.

### D — COMPARE_VISUAL
Two states or choices shown in one coherent frame or split composition. May use people, objects or both.

### E — TEXT_ILLUSTRATION
A short term or statement integrated into a strong illustration. **No human required.**

## 8. Target mix

For a typical psychology video, use variety without forcing exact math:
- ~50–60% CHARACTER_SCENE
- ~15–20% SCHEMA_EXPLAINER
- ~15–20% OBJECT_METAPHOR
- ~5–15% COMPARE_VISUAL
- up to ~10% TEXT_ILLUSTRATION

These are guidance only. The best representation for the beat wins.

**Do not force a stickman into every scene.**

## 9. One clear beat per frame

Every image communicates one main thought.

Required:
- clear focal subject or visual relation
- scene matches the current spoken beat
- environmental details support the point
- no unrelated future information
- no visual clutter merely to look expensive

## 10. Character individuality and continuity

Same world does NOT mean same person.

Individualize scene-appropriate characters through:
- clothing color/style
- simple hair cue
- glasses
- bag/accessory
- modest height/proportion variation within the same family
- posture and expression

When the same person continues across nearby scenes, preserve their clothing, head design, accessory and proportions.

Individuality must never be created by switching to a different anatomy/rendering family.

## 12. Compact global prompt

`Create a cinematic 2D illustrated frame for a modern psychology YouTube channel. Keep one consistent film-world. UNIFIED HUMAN CAST: every visible human — main, supporting and background — uses the same stylized stickman-inspired family with large simple round heads, minimal expressive faces, compact clothed torsos, very slim simplified limbs, simple hands and shoes. Individualize only through clothing, simple hair/accessories, size, posture and expression; never mix normal cartoon humans or another anatomy family. HUMAN PRESENCE IS OPTIONAL: when a person is not needed, use schemas, object metaphors, comparisons, environment illustrations or text illustrations in the exact same linework, color, soft cel shading, depth and lighting language. ## 13. Text inside the world

Normal character/object scenes should remain text-free unless text is necessary.

Planned text modes:
- cover title
- keyword
- schema title + up to three short labels
- comparison labels
- text-illustration phrase

All wording is exact German text from the scene plan. No random readable background text.

## 14. Cover rule

Cover uses the same world but stronger composition:
- one dominant emotional conflict or question
- stronger contrast
- clearer silhouette
- more deliberate lighting
- integrated safe title area
- still looks like the same illustrator/world as normal frames

If multiple people appear on the cover, **all of them must use the same stickman family**.

Do not switch to a separate comic/poster/cartoon style just for the cover.

## 15. Hard fail

Regenerate if:
- any human becomes a bare line-stickman/whiteboard doodle
- any human becomes a normal full cartoon, semi-realistic or realistic person
- main character is stickman-inspired but supporting/background people use another human style
- crowd contains mixed anatomy families
- generic corporate-vector humans appear
- 3D/CGI/Pixar/anime drift
- environment becomes photorealistic
- frame looks like corporate stock vector art
- schema looks like a PowerPoint instead of part of the illustrated world
- rendering/line language changes materially between scenes
- a decorative human is added where a clearer no-human visual would work better
- random readable text appears
- current speech beat is not visually clear
