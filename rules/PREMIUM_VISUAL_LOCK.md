## What premium quality means

Premium does **not** mean photorealistic or over-detailed.

It means:
- polished hand-drawn 2D Stickman illustration
- clear expressive characters
- one strong visual idea per frame
- clean dark outlines
- simple readable shapes
- flat controlled colors
- soft graphic/cel shading
- useful depth and composition
- a scene world that matches the topic
- characters and environment rendered in the same illustration language

## Character quality

Use simple round/oval heads, slim simplified bodies, thin clearly drawn limbs, simple clothing, readable gestures and restrained facial expression.

Do not force hairline doodle limbs or mathematically tiny torsos. Characters must remain natural enough to sit, kneel, point, carry and interact.

## Environment quality

Use recognizable but simplified 2D environments. Detail supports the current beat.

Avoid the mismatch of a simple Stickman placed inside a semi-realistic 3D/product-render-looking room.

## Topic-specific mood

The same channel style can create different worlds:
- bright/open
- cool/uneasy
- dark/powerful
- commercial/money
- sparse/isolated
- warm/hopeful

Do not force the same red/dark or teal/cream palette into every video.

## Composition

Use camera and spacing to make the current idea immediately readable. A background element stays only when it helps context, scale, continuity or attention.

## Minimal QA fail

Regenerate when:
- the human becomes realistic, chibi, 3D/Pixar/anime or bare whiteboard doodle
- the environment looks like a different rendering medium from the characters
- the scene is cluttered or the current beat is unclear
- random readable text appears
- later batches visibly reset the art style

`rules/VISUAL_SYSTEM.md` is authoritative.
