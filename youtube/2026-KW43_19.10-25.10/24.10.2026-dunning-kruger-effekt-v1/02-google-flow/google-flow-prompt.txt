STYLE (applies to every image)
Hand-drawn 2D illustration: dark slightly organic outlines, soft cel shading, subtle paper texture, natural clear colors, real depth. People are stickmen with a large round white head, tiny black oval eyes, small simple eyebrows and mouth, a narrow clothed body and very thin dark stick-like arms and legs. All people in one image share exactly this build. Never add text, letters, numbers, signs or captions unless a BILD gives exact German text — then write exactly that text and nothing else.

CHARACTERS (create a character sheet first with vorlagen/figuren/02-figuren-sheet.txt)
- Wheeler: adult stickman, large white round head, tiny black oval eyes, short dark stubble beard, grey hooded sweatshirt, dark jeans, very thin dark stick limbs
- Jonas: young adult stickman, messy brown hair, green hoodie, light grey trousers, sneakers
- Vater (Jonas' father): older stickman, short grey hair, round glasses, beige cardigan
- Zuschauer-Figur: ordinary stickman, short dark hair, plain blue sweater, appears at mirror, sofa and crossroad

RECURRING METAPHOR
The doubt lamp: a small round lamp that floats above a person's head on a thin cable. It glows warm yellow when the person notices uncertainty, and stays dark when they feel sure without reason. Create a metaphor sheet first (vorlagen/figuren/03-metapher-sheet.txt) with the lamp dark, flickering, glowing and unplugged.
