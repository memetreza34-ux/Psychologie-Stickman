STYLE HARD LOCK — UNIVERSAL EDITORIAL STICKMAN WORLD v1.3
STRICT STICKMAN ONLY.

EVERY visible human or human-like figure must use the same TRUE STICKMAN DNA, but they do NOT need to be the same person.

TRUE STICKMAN ANATOMY — NON-NEGOTIABLE
- simple round or oval flat head
- tiny minimal black eyes and simple mouth
- no realistic nose
- no realistic ears
- no realistic neck
- small strongly simplified torso
- very thin line-like arms and legs with near-constant thickness
- no fleshy upper arms, forearms, thighs or calves
- simple nub/mitten/circle hands, no finger anatomy
- simplified feet/shoes
- clothing may identify role or situation but must stay wrapped around the simplified stickman structure
- clothing must NEVER create normal human/cartoon anatomy

HARD FAIL
- conventional cartoon humans
- semi-realistic humans
- realistic humans
- thick human-like limbs
- detailed fingers/hands
- realistic noses/ears/faces
- mixed human styles
- 3D/CGI/Pixar
- anime/manga/chibi

ENVIRONMENT RULE
The world can be rich and detailed: believable homes, offices, libraries, cafes, kitchens, classrooms, buses, trains, streets, parks, workshops, lounges, phones, clocks, windows, lamps, notebooks, shelves, soft cinematic light and clear perspective. Detail belongs in the environment, NOT in human anatomy.

CHANNEL DNA — HARD LOCK
Simple Stickmen. Rich World. One clear idea per image. German visual language.
- Every image must communicate ONE primary visual thought from the exact current speech beat.
- Use ONE dominant focal point.
- Normally use only 1–2 supporting visual cues.
- Prefer concrete everyday stickman actions over abstract infographic metaphors whenever possible.
- Avoid split-screen, comic panels, collages, dashboards and dense infographics.
- If a scene becomes overloaded, simplify it automatically while preserving the exact meaning.
- Vary camera distance, pose, environment, cast and composition naturally between scenes.
- Do not turn the video into repeated cards, meters, scales or diagrams.

CAST VARIATION — HARD LOCK
STYLE CONSISTENCY DOES NOT MEAN CHARACTER CLONING.
- 01.png is a STYLE AND ANATOMY reference, NOT a character identity template for the whole video.
- Use different stickman people when the current beat does not require narrative continuity.
- Vary clothing colors and outfit types, simple accessories, role cues, cast size, pose, location and camera angle.
- Choose 1 to 4 Stickmen when the scene benefits from them.
- Roles may include student, office worker, commuter, friend, researcher or everyday person.
- Do NOT default to the same lone teal-hoodie Stickman.
- Do NOT default to the same desk, same home-office room, same pose or same medium shot.
- Reuse an exact character only inside an explicitly connected mini-story marked LOCAL CONTINUITY.

BEFORE EACH SCENE DECIDE
1. CAST — who/how many Stickmen best explain this beat?
2. LOCATION — which believable place makes the idea clearest?
3. ACTION — what visible action represents the spoken meaning?
4. CAMERA — what angle/distance makes the action instantly readable?

FLOW SOURCE IMAGE TEXT POLICY — ZERO READABLE TEXT
Generated source images must contain ZERO readable text.
Do not render readable German text.
Do not render readable English text.
Do not render UI text, labels, buttons, posters, subtitles, notes, headlines, logos or document text.
Do not copy the German speech context into the image.

German labels for the final video are added later in Remotion, not inside the source images.
If visible text is ever explicitly allowed as an exception, it must be exact German text only; English visible text is always forbidden.
