# Quellen & Claim-Check — Planungsfehlschluss

## Kernthema
Planning fallacy = people tend to make completion-time predictions for their own tasks that are too optimistic when they focus on a plan/scenario rather than relevant past experience. It is **not** a synonym for procrastination or for every delay.

## Primärquellen

1. Buehler, R., Griffin, D., & Ross, M. (1994). *Exploring the "Planning Fallacy": Why People Underestimate Their Task Completion Times.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 67(3), 366–381. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.67.3.366
   - Full text hosted by MIT: https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/biases/67_J_Personality_and_Social_Psychology_366,_1994.pdf
   - Study 1: 37 surveyed honours thesis students; 33 analyzed on-time outcomes; mean best prediction 33.9 days vs actual mean 55.5 days; not a universal multiplier.
   - The study also tested think-aloud forecasts, past experiences and observer predictions across five studies. Broad tendency, not every individual's fate.
   - Caution: recall-relevance manipulation reduced directional bias but did **not** necessarily improve absolute prediction accuracy.

2. Kahneman, D. & Lovallo, D. (1993). *Timid Choices and Bold Forecasts: A Cognitive Perspective on Risk Taking.* Management Science 39(1), 17–31. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.39.1.17
   - Publisher abstract: https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/mnsc.39.1.17
   - Inside view: case-specific imagined future and plan. Outside view: distribution of comparable completed projects, allowing corrections.

## Script-Claims / Quellen-Gate

- "34 Tage vs 56 Tage" = **rounding** of study sample means, not an experimental treatment effect; describe "im Durchschnitt" and "in einer Untersuchung".
- "Many describe the planned path rather than recalling their past" = supported by the study's think-aloud protocols.
- "Outsider forecasts may use past experience" = supported as a tendency from observer comparison, **not** as a universal superiority of observers.
- "Record previous task timings; compare similar projects" = editorial practical recommendation drawing on outside-view logic; **not** a claim of guaranteed improvement.
- Shelf assembly, moving friend and video editing are **constructed illustrative examples**, not real study subjects or evidence for numeric frequencies.
- Some underestimates occur for genuine changes and unforeseen demands: no claim that all delays are personal cognitive biases.
- No names/years spoken in script to avoid bibliography narration; full sources in upload description.

## Headline gate
"Nur 2 Stunden?" and "Warum du ständig unterschätzt ..." are explanatory hooks. Neither implies every project must overrun.
