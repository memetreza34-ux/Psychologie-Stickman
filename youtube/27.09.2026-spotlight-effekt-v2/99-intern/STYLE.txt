Use CINEMATIC_ILLUSTRATED_PSYCHOLOGY_V1 exactly.

UNIFIED HUMAN CAST — HARD LOCK
Every visible human — main, supporting and background — uses the same stylized stickman-inspired character family: large simple round/oval heads, minimal expressive faces, compact clothed torsos, very slim simplified limbs, simple hands and shoes.

Supporting passengers may be less detailed, but must never become normal cartoon humans, semi-realistic people, realistic people or generic vector humans. Individualize only through clothing, simple hair/accessory, modest size variation, pose and expression.

CHARACTER PRESENCE — OPTIONAL
Not every scene needs a person. Schemas, object metaphors, symbolic illustrations, comparisons and text illustrations may be completely human-free when clearer. Do not insert decorative people merely to fill space.

WORLD
Colorful believable 2D environments, clean slightly organic outlines, soft cel shading, natural depth, controlled warm/cool lighting and small meaningful details.

No bare line stickmen, mixed human character families, normal cartoon humans, 3D/Pixar, anime, photorealism, corporate vector look or whiteboard style.

Schemas, metaphors and text illustrations must still feel like frames from the same animated film.
