STYLE — CINEMATIC ILLUSTRATED PSYCHOLOGY WORLD v1

UNIFIED HUMAN CAST — HARD LOCK
- every visible human uses the same stylized stickman-inspired family
- applies to main, supporting and background people, crowds and memory inserts
- large simple round/oval head, minimal expressive face
- compact clothed torso
- very slim simplified limbs with slight width
- simple hands and shoes
- individualize only through clothing, color, hair cue, glasses/accessory, modest size variation, pose and expression
- background people may be simpler, but never a different human anatomy/rendering style

CHARACTER PRESENCE — OPTIONAL
- not every frame needs a person
- use a character only when human action/reaction helps explain the beat
- schemas, object metaphors, object close-ups, comparison visuals and text illustrations may contain no humans
- never add a decorative person only to fill empty space

WORLD
- colorful believable everyday locations
- clean slightly organic outlines
- soft cel shading
- natural depth
- cinematic illustrated warm/cool lighting
- meaningful small details

ALLOWED NON-CHARACTER FRAMES
- illustrated schemas
- object metaphors
- comparison visuals
- text illustrations
- symbolic environment/object close-ups
All use the same world/rendering language.

HARD FAIL
- main stickman + normal cartoon/realistic side characters
- mixed human character families inside one frame
- whiteboard/bare stickman
- normal full cartoon or realistic humans
- generic corporate-vector humans
- 3D/Pixar/anime
- photorealistic environment
- corporate infographic look

CHARACTER SHAPE LOCK — PLANUNGSFALLACY_CLOTHED_STICKMAN_V1
Planungsfehlschluss-based clothed stickman family: oversized warm-white round/soft-oval head, tiny solid black oval eyes, expressive brows and a simple mouth; a narrow simplified torso, very thin dark limbs and small simplified hands/shoes. Clothing wraps the existing slender construction with restrained fabric volume; it does not create ordinary human shoulders, hips or thighs. Keep the same illustrated anatomy for every visible person. A torso may be a simple filled white or clothed shape; it is NOT required to be a single line. Hair and accessories individualize people without replacing the pale head with an ordinary human face.
Keep the head visually dominant, with a pale face and restrained flat edge shadow. Use mature expressive posture, not chibi proportions. Clothed bodies may have narrow filled shapes; do not force single-line torsos. Detailed drawn spatial environments share the character linework, shading and material treatment. Individual cast per episode, consistent species and illustrator across the channel.
If an accepted original Planungsfehlschluss frame or the accepted narrow-jacket test is already available in Flow, use it as a construction/finish reference during initial cast design without copying its identity, pose or room. No externally generated Codex reference image is required. Then use the final approved cover as style reference for every scene, with only necessary identity assets.
Visually check initial references and all three covers for this construction before numbered scenes. Reject normal cartoon-human anatomy and bare whiteboard-doodle substitutions.
