GLOBAL WORLD — HARD LOCK
Create a cinematic 2D illustrated frame for a modern psychology YouTube channel. Keep one consistent film-world with colorful believable environments, clean slightly organic outlines, soft cel shading, natural depth, purposeful cinematic framing, controlled colors, warm/cool lighting and small meaningful details. No photorealism, no 3D/Pixar, no anime, no corporate vector look, no whiteboard doodle style.

UNIFIED HUMAN CAST — HARD LOCK
Whenever humans appear, EVERY visible human must belong to the SAME stylized stickman-inspired family.
This includes main character, supporting characters, background passengers/crowds, tiny memory figures and depicted humans on screens/posters/reflections.
Family DNA for every human:
- large simple round/oval head
- minimal expressive face
- compact clothed torso
- very slim simplified illustrated arms and legs with slight width
- simple hands
- simple shoes/feet
Supporting/background people may use less detail, but they MUST NOT become normal cartoon humans, semi-realistic people, realistic people, generic corporate-vector people or another anatomy family.
Individualize only through clothing, colors, simple hair cue, glasses/accessory, modest size variation, posture and expression.
Never mix one good stickman main character with differently styled human side characters.

CHARACTER PRESENCE — OPTIONAL HARD LOCK
A person is NOT required in every frame.
Choose the representation that explains the current beat most clearly.
When people do not add explanatory value, prefer a no-human frame such as:
- SCHEMA_EXPLAINER
- OBJECT_METAPHOR
- object close-up
- symbolic environment
- COMPARE_VISUAL using objects/states
- TEXT_ILLUSTRATION
Do not insert decorative stickmen just to fill space.
No-human frames must still use the same linework, colors, soft cel shading, depth, lighting and overall film-world.

SCENE TYPES
- CHARACTER_SCENE: relatable human action in a believable place; all humans use unified stickman family
- SCHEMA_EXPLAINER: illustrated mechanism/relationship; human optional
- OBJECT_METAPHOR: symbolic object/environment; no human required
- COMPARE_VISUAL: two states/options in one coherent frame; human optional
- TEXT_ILLUSTRATION: short phrase integrated into a strong illustration; human optional

Target mix is roughly 50–60% character scenes with the rest schemas, metaphors, comparisons and text/object illustrations. This is guidance only; clarity wins.

WORLD CONSISTENCY
Same world does not mean same room or person. Vary character identity, clothing, location, camera and lighting according to the beat. Repeat a person only for genuine continuity. Non-character images must still look as if created by the same illustrator for the same animated film.

ENVIRONMENT + CAMERA
Use recognizable real locations and meaningful details. Plan foreground/midground/background when useful. Choose medium, wide, close, side, over-shoulder, high or low angle according to storytelling. Background detail must support, not compete.
