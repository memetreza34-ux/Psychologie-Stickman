STYLE HARD LOCK — UNIVERSAL EDITORIAL STICKMAN WORLD v1.3
STRICT STICKMAN ONLY.
Every visible human or human-like figure must use true stickman anatomy: simple round/oval head, tiny minimal face, no realistic nose/ears/neck, small simplified torso, very thin line-like arms and legs, simple no-finger hands, simplified feet. RICH WORLD
Use believable detailed 2D environments, natural colors, clean dark organic outlines, subtle cel shading, believable perspective and lighting. Detail belongs in the environment, not in human anatomy.

CHANNEL DNA — HARD LOCK

VISUAL VARIETY — HARD LOCK
Vary composition families across the whole video.

- roughly 70% standard concrete scenes
- roughly 20% split/contrast scenes
- roughly 10% multi-panel/montage scenes

Use montage when a main scene plus 1–2 supporting mini-scenes or examples of the SAME spoken beat improve understanding.
Do not default to one-stickman-in-room compositions.

Do not force extra people into genuinely internal solo beats.
