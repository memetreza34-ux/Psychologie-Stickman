STYLE HARD LOCK — UNIVERSAL EDITORIAL STICKMAN WORLD v1.3
STRICT STICKMAN ONLY.

EVERY visible human or human-like figure must use the exact same true stickman anatomy, including foreground people, background people, crowds, researchers, historical figures, people on screens, posters, photos, advertisements, reflections and illustrations inside the scene.

TRUE STICKMAN ANATOMY:
- simple round or oval flat head
- tiny minimal eyes and mouth
- no realistic nose
- no realistic ears
- no realistic neck
- small strongly simplified torso
- very thin line-like arms and legs with near-constant thickness
- no fleshy human/cartoon limb volume
- simple nub/mitten/circle hands, no finger anatomy
- simplified feet/shoes
- clothing may identify role, era or personality but must never create normal human anatomy

HARD FAIL:
- conventional cartoon humans
- semi-realistic humans
- realistic humans
- thick human-like arms or legs
- detailed hands/fingers
- realistic face anatomy
- main character as stickman while background characters look human
- 3D/CGI/Pixar
- anime/manga/chibi

ENVIRONMENT RULE
The environment may be much richer and more detailed than the characters: believable rooms, buses, trains, streets, laboratories, furniture, devices, architecture, props, lighting and perspective. Detail belongs in the world, not in human anatomy.

GLOBAL SCENE CLAUSE — APPLIES TO EVERY SINGLE SCENE EVEN IF NOT REPEATED BELOW
STRICT STICKMAN-ONLY CHECK: Every person visible anywhere in the foreground, midground, background, screens, posters, reflections, photos or illustrations must use the same true stickman anatomy as the approved master. Do not generate normal cartoon humans. If any person looks human-like rather than stickman, the image fails and must be regenerated.

VISIBLE TEXT — GERMAN ONLY + MINIMAL
This is a German-language channel. The prompt language may be English, but the visible language inside the generated image must remain German.
- prefer NO readable text whenever the idea can be shown visually
- if text is necessary: German only
- never render English words, headings, labels, buttons, UI, posters, cards or screen text
- do not copy English wording from this prompt into the image
- normally use only one short German label or phrase
- target 1–4 visible words
- avoid full sentences, paragraphs, explanation cards and text-heavy layouts
- if a scene below asks for too much text, shorten/translate it or replace it with visual storytelling
- this rule OVERRIDES any conflicting text instruction in a scene prompt

AUTOMATIC BATCH WORKFLOW — HARD LOCK

- niemals alle Bilder gleichzeitig erzeugen
- immer höchstens 5 neue Bilder pro Batch
- nach jedem Batch interne Style-, Continuity-, Inhalts- und Text-QA durchführen
- jedes Bild einzeln gegen 01.png prüfen
- zusätzlich den gesamten Batch untereinander vergleichen
- alle sichtbaren Personen inklusive Mini-Hintergrundfiguren auf STRICT STICKMAN ONLY prüfen
