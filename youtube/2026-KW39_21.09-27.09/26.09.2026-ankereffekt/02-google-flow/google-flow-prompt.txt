VISUAL VARIETY — HARD LOCK
Do not repeat the same one-Stickman-in-a-room composition.
Target across a 35-scene video: about 70% standard connected scenes, about 20% split/contrast comparisons, about 10% montage/multi-panel scenes.
A montage may contain one dominant main scene plus at most 1–2 supporting sub-scenes, but every sub-scene must explain the SAME spoken beat.
Vary camera distance, angle, foreground/background depth, location, prop logic, cast size and body pose.

SOCIAL SCENE VARIETY — HARD LOCK
Use SOLO only when one person is semantically best.
Use DUO for negotiations, comparisons, feedback and interpersonal influence.
Use GROUP for experiments, social estimates, workshops and multiple-person judgment situations.
Multi-person scenes need real interaction/reaction, not people standing side by side.

CAST VARIATION — HARD LOCK
Same Stickman DNA does not mean same character.
Use new fitting people, roles, clothing and color accents across unrelated scenes.
