STYLE HARD LOCK — UNIVERSAL EDITORIAL STICKMAN WORLD v1.3
STRICT STICKMAN ONLY.

EVERY visible human or human-like figure must use TRUE STICKMAN anatomy. They do NOT need to be the same person.

TRUE STICKMAN ANATOMY — NON-NEGOTIABLE
- simple round or oval flat head, slightly smaller than mascot proportions
- tiny black dot eyes and very small simple mouth
- no realistic nose
- no realistic ears
- no realistic neck
- small strongly simplified torso
- very thin line-like arms and legs with near-constant thickness
- no fleshy upper arms, forearms, thighs or calves
- simple rounded nub/mitten/circle hands, no finger anatomy
- simplified feet/shoes
- clothing may identify role or situation but must stay wrapped around the simplified stickman structure
- clothing must NEVER create normal human/cartoon anatomy

HARD FAIL
- conventional cartoon humans
- semi-realistic or realistic humans
- thick human-like limbs
- detailed fingers/hands
- realistic noses/ears/faces
- mixed human styles
- 3D/CGI/Pixar
- anime/manga/chibi
- mascot-like oversized heads

ENVIRONMENT RULE
The world may be rich, detailed and believable: rooms, cafés, streets, trains, seminar rooms, parks, mirrors, shops, presentation spaces, furniture, plants, windows and soft cinematic light. Detail belongs in the environment, NOT in human anatomy.

CHANNEL DNA — HARD LOCK
