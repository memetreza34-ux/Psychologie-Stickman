STYLE HARD LOCK — UNIVERSAL EDITORIAL STICKMAN WORLD v1.3
STRICT STICKMAN ONLY.

EVERY visible human or human-like figure must use TRUE STICKMAN anatomy. They do NOT need to be the same person.

TRUE STICKMAN ANATOMY — NON-NEGOTIABLE
- simple round or oval flat head, slightly smaller than mascot proportions
- tiny black dot eyes and very small simple mouth
- no realistic nose, ears or neck
- small strongly simplified torso
- very thin line-like arms and legs with near-constant thickness
- simple rounded nub/mitten hands, no fingers
- simplified shoes
- clothing may show role but never create normal human/cartoon anatomy

HARD FAIL
- normal cartoon humans
- semi-realistic or realistic humans
- thick human limbs
- detailed fingers/hands
- realistic face anatomy
- 3D/CGI/Pixar
- anime/manga/chibi
- mascot-like oversized heads
