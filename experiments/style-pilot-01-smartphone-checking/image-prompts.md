# Finale Bildprompts — Style Pilot 01

## MASTER PREFIX — vor jeden Szenenprompt setzen

`Universal editorial 2D stickman explainer world, 16:9, 1920x1080. ALL visible human characters must use the exact same minimalist stickman anatomy: simple round head, tiny minimal eyes and mouth, thin dark line arms and legs, small simplified torso, simple hands, no realistic anatomy. This applies to main characters, background people, people on screens and small distant figures. Richer grounded environment, clean dark organic outlines, natural object colors, moderate-to-vivid controlled saturation, believable perspective and lighting, simplified 2D materials, subtle cel shading, educational visual storytelling. The environment may be realistic in structure and detail, but every human remains unmistakably stickman.`

## HARD NEGATIVE — an jeden Prompt anhängen

`No normal cartoon humans, no semi-realistic people, no realistic faces, no realistic arms or legs, no detailed fingers, no mixed human styles, no 3D, no CGI, no anime, no chibi, no photorealism, no washed-out beige filter, no generic whiteboard emptiness, no brand logos, no unnecessary text blocks.`

---

## 01 — Nur kurz die Uhrzeit

`Grounded modern 2026 bedroom/home-office scene. A young adult stickman sits at a desk with an open laptop and notebook, lifts a modern smartphone only to check the lock-screen time. The phone and the quick glance are the visual focus. Contemporary furniture, charging cable, headphones, desk lamp, books and a window with daylight. Natural blue, green, warm wood and small orange accents. Medium detail, believable perspective.`

## 02 — Zwei Minuten später in der App

`Same stickman, same room, same clothes and same desk continuity. Two minutes later the character is slouched deeper in the chair, scrolling through a colorful short-form content feed while the unfinished laptop task remains open and ignored. Strong visual contrast between bright phone screen and unattended work. Modern, relatable 2026 atmosphere.`

## 03 — Nicht einfach Disziplin

`Concept/abstract psychology scene. The same stickman stands between two visual explanations. On the left, a large accusing pointing-hand symbol and a rigid checklist represent the simplistic idea of “lack of discipline”. On the right, a calm circular habit pathway with repeated phone-check actions represents a behavioral mechanism. Visually favor the neutral habit explanation without large text labels.`

## 04 — Wiederholte Checking-Habit

`Concept-to-everyday hybrid scene. Show the same stickman across four small connected moments in one frame: phone in pocket, quick glance at phone, phone back in pocket, then another quick glance later. The repeated action should read instantly as brief checking rather than long phone use. Clean sequence, lively but controlled colors, no duplicate normal humans.`

## 05 — Neue Information verstärkt den Check

`Grounded modern editorial scene focused on the smartphone. The same stickman checks the phone and sees several different new-information cards arriving over time: message preview, weather update, social update and news-style card, all generic with no logos. Use stronger color on new information while the surrounding room stays slightly quieter. The stickman’s body language shows renewed attention.`

## 06 — Ton oder Vibration

`Modern office/home-office scene. The stickman is focused on a laptop when a smartphone lying to the side visibly vibrates on the table with small motion lines. The character has not touched it yet, but the head and eyes immediately turn toward the phone. Bright natural daylight, realistic desk objects and clear blue/green/orange accents.`

## 07 — Aufmerksamkeitsexperiment

`Grounded simplified laboratory/test-room scene. A stickman participant performs a demanding attention task on a computer monitor while an untouched smartphone lies beside the keyboard and receives a notification. Show the moment of distraction through gaze shift and a slightly broken focus line toward the task. Any researcher or background participant must also be the same stickman type.`

## 08 — Der Kreislauf

`Abstract process illustration inside the same stickman visual world. Build a four-step circular pathway: trigger in the environment, stickman hand reaches for phone, new information appears on screen, loop returns to trigger. Integrate a small stickman into the process instead of making a corporate infographic. Strong clear arrows, controlled red/blue/yellow/green accents, off-white only because this is an abstract scene.`

## 09 — Gewohnheit ist nicht automatisch Sucht

`Concept comparison scene. Split the frame into two clearly separated visual paths. Left: an everyday stickman repeatedly checking a phone in normal daily situations. Right: a caution area with a large question mark and medical-warning symbolism, deliberately not diagnosing the stickman. The composition must communicate that habitual checking and addiction are not automatically the same thing. Keep the tone neutral and scientific.`

## 10 — Forschung ist komplex

`Editorial research scene. A stickman researcher stands at a large desk and wall board with several simplified study papers, charts and evidence cards. Some connections are solid and clearly linked; others are dotted or marked with small question marks to show uncertainty. Other visible researchers in the background must also be minimalist stickmen. Modern university/research environment, rich natural colors.`

## 11 — Automatischer Griff

`Grounded modern city everyday scene. A stickman walks along a realistic contemporary sidewalk past shops, bicycles and other stickman pedestrians. The character’s attention is directed forward, but one hand is already moving almost automatically toward the smartphone in the trouser pocket. Use natural urban colors, trees, blue sky, red and yellow street accents. Every background person must remain unmistakably stickman.`

## 12 — Vor der bewussten Entscheidung

`Cinematic conceptual closing shot. The same stickman pauses with one hand touching the phone but not yet pulling it out. Behind the character, two subtle illustrated paths diverge: one fast curved path representing automatic impulse and one slower deliberate path representing conscious choice. No large labels required. Rich dark-blue and warm amber lighting, visually calm ending, strong character silhouette while keeping exact stickman anatomy.`

---

## Generierungsregel

Für jedes Bild gilt: `MASTER PREFIX + Szenenprompt + HARD NEGATIVE`.

Wenn auch nur eine deutlich sichtbare menschliche Figur als normaler Cartoon-Mensch statt Stickman erscheint, Bild verwerfen und neu generieren. Nicht nachträglich als „gut genug“ akzeptieren.
