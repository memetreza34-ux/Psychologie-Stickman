# Finale Bildprompts — Style Pilot 01 v2

## MASTER PREFIX — vor jeden Szenenprompt setzen

`Universal editorial 2D stickman explainer world, 16:9, 1920x1080. ALL visible human characters must use the exact same minimalist stickman anatomy: simple round head, tiny minimal eyes and mouth, thin dark line arms and legs, small simplified torso, simple hands, no realistic anatomy. This applies to main characters, background people, people on screens and small distant figures. Rich grounded environment, clean dark organic outlines, natural object colors, moderate-to-vivid controlled saturation, believable perspective and lighting, simplified 2D materials, subtle cel shading, educational visual storytelling. The environment may be realistic in structure and detail, but every human remains unmistakably stickman.`

## HARD NEGATIVE — an jeden Prompt anhängen

`No normal cartoon humans, no semi-realistic people, no realistic faces, no realistic arms or legs, no detailed fingers, no mixed human styles, no 3D, no CGI, no anime, no chibi, no photorealism, no washed-out beige filter, no generic whiteboard emptiness, no brand logos, no unnecessary text blocks.`

## 01 — Nur kurz die Uhrzeit
`Tight modern close-up. A stickman lifts a smartphone only to check the lock-screen time. The time display is the clear focus, the face is simple and neutral, background home-office elements are softly simplified. Cool daylight, dark phone frame, one warm accent.`

## 02 — Ausgangssituation
`Grounded 2026 home-office wide shot. The same stickman sits at a desk with an open laptop, notebook and smartphone resting beside it. Modern chair, headphones, charging cable, plant, lamp and window. Natural blue, green, warm wood and orange accents. Clean believable spatial layout.`

## 03 — Entsperren
`Extreme detail shot of the same stickman hand unlocking the smartphone. Keep the simplified stickman hand design, dark organic outlines and clean modern screen glow. Background reduced to laptop edge and desk surface. Strong visual focus, no text clutter.`

## 04 — Zwei Minuten später
`Same room and same stickman continuity. The character is now slouched deeper in the chair scrolling through a colorful generic short-form content feed. The phone screen is bright and attention-grabbing. Contemporary room, vivid but controlled color palette.`

## 05 — Aufgabe bleibt offen
`Over-the-shoulder composition. The unfinished laptop task fills the foreground while the same stickman in the middle ground is absorbed in the smartphone. The visual contrast must instantly show abandoned work versus phone attention. Modern believable workspace.`

## 06 — Schlechte Disziplin?
`Abstract psychology scene. The stickman stands beneath an oversized accusing pointing-hand symbol and a rigid checklist, visually representing the simplistic judgment of poor discipline. Strong composition, off-white abstract background, restrained red accent, no large text.`

## 07 — Forschende untersuchen das
`Modern editorial research scene. Three stickman researchers sit around a table with smartphones, notes, timers and simple charts. Every researcher uses the exact same stickman anatomy. Contemporary university lab, rich natural colors, blue screens, green plants, warm wood.`

## 08 — Wiederholte Checks
`Sequence illustration with the same stickman shown in four connected mini-moments: waiting, quick phone glance, phone away, later another quick glance. The environments subtly shift to show time passing while keeping one coherent visual world.`

## 09 — Handy raus
`Dynamic waist-up urban shot. A walking stickman pulls a smartphone from a trouser or jacket pocket. Clear motion line, realistic contemporary sidewalk, shop windows, bicycles and traffic elements in the background. All background people are stickmen.`

## 10 — Kurz schauen
`Profile close-up of the stickman face and smartphone. The character gives one quick glance at the screen. Minimal facial features, strong eye direction, clean dark outlines, shallow visual complexity, vivid screen against quieter background.`

## 11 — Wieder weg
`Close shot of the same stickman putting the smartphone back into the pocket while continuing to walk. The arm moves downward, environment remains active. Use a different camera angle from scene 09 to avoid repetition.`

## 12 — Wenig später nochmal
`Modern public transport stop. The same stickman stands waiting and checks the phone again. Digital timetable, bench, shelter, city street and several background stickman commuters. Strong modern colors, blue signage, green trees, red and yellow accents.`

## 13 — Neue Informationen
`Grounded modern scene focused on the phone. Several generic information cards appear sequentially on the screen while the stickman immediately refocuses on it. Use stronger saturation on new information and slightly quieter colors in the room.`

## 14 — Nachricht, Update, Neues
`Three-part micro-composition in one frame connected by the same stickman hand and smartphone: a message preview, a simple update card, and another generic new-information card. No logos. Distinct blue, green and orange accents with clear hierarchy.`

## 15 — Noch passiert nichts
`Quiet suspenseful everyday scene. The smartphone lies inactive on a modern desk while the stickman works on the laptop. Calm daylight, balanced composition, no notification yet. The image should feel still and focused, preparing contrast for the next beat.`

## 16 — Ton oder Vibration
`Same workspace. The smartphone visibly vibrates with subtle motion lines and a tiny sound cue. The stickman has not touched it, but the head and eyes turn immediately away from the laptop toward the device. Clear attention shift, stronger accent on the phone.`

## 17 — Aufmerksamkeitsexperiment
`Grounded simplified laboratory scene. A stickman participant performs a demanding attention task on a computer monitor. Clean modern test room, neutral desk, keyboard, simple test graphics. Any researcher in the background is also an unmistakable stickman.`

## 18 — Handy bleibt unberührt
`Same laboratory from a different angle. The smartphone lies untouched beside the keyboard and receives a notification while the participant’s hands remain on the computer. Show distraction through gaze shift only. No one touches the phone.`

## 19 — Der Kreislauf entsteht
`Abstract concept scene. Build a clean circular path with four empty stations around a small central stickman, visually preparing a habit loop without yet filling every station. Strong arrows, controlled blue, yellow, red and green accents, off-white background.`

## 20 — Auslöser und Griff
`First half of the loop. A vibrating smartphone or notification cue acts as the trigger, followed by a simplified stickman hand reaching toward the phone. Two clearly separated visual steps connected by a bold directional arrow.`

## 21 — Information und Wiederholung
`Second half of the loop. A new colorful information card appears on the smartphone, then a large curved arrow returns to the beginning of the loop. The closed cycle should be readable instantly without corporate infographic styling.`

## 22 — Gewohnheit ist nicht Sucht
`Neutral scientific comparison scene. Left side: ordinary stickman repeatedly checking a phone in normal daily life. Right side: a clearly separate caution zone with a question mark and restrained medical-warning symbolism. Do not diagnose or dramatize.`

## 23 — Forschung ist komplex
`Editorial evidence scene. Several stickman researchers examine study pages, charts and evidence cards. Some links between findings are solid, others dotted or uncertain. Modern research environment, rich color, strong visual hierarchy, no normal cartoon people.`

## 24 — Automatisches Verhalten
`Grounded modern city or subway scene. The stickman looks forward at the environment while one hand already drifts toward the smartphone pocket almost automatically. Other visible people are stickmen. Natural urban colors and believable lighting.`

## 25 — Hand zur Tasche
`Cinematic close-up of torso, arm and pocket only. The stickman hand moves toward the smartphone in the pocket while the head is outside the frame or looking elsewhere. Strong dark outline, realistic clothing folds in simplified 2D form, no realistic anatomy.`

## 26 — Vor der bewussten Entscheidung
`Calm cinematic closing shot. The stickman pauses with one hand resting at the phone pocket, not yet pulling it out. Behind the figure, two subtle illustrated paths diverge: one fast curved impulse path and one slower deliberate path. Dark blue evening tones with warm amber highlights. Strong final composition, no large text.`

## Generierungsregel

Für jedes Bild gilt: `MASTER PREFIX + Szenenprompt + HARD NEGATIVE`.

Die 26 Bilder sollen **nicht** wie 26 Varianten derselben Szene aussehen. Perspektive, Kameradistanz, Umgebung und Darstellungsmodus müssen sinnvoll wechseln: Wide Shot, Close-up, Over-the-shoulder, Detailshot, Research Scene, Concept Scene, Process Scene und Environmental Scene.

Wenn auch nur eine deutlich sichtbare menschliche Figur als normaler Cartoon-Mensch statt Stickman erscheint, Bild verwerfen und neu generieren. Keine schwache Komposition nur wegen der Bildanzahl akzeptieren.
